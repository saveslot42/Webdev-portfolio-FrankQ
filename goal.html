Sustainable Development Goal 3: Good Health and Well-Being

The United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 3 aims to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages by 2030.

This goal addresses critical health challenges, including reducing maternal and child mortality, combating infectious diseases like HIV/AIDS, and ensuring universal access to healthcare services.

It emphasizes the importance of mental health, substance abuse prevention, and strengthening health systems in all countries.

Achieving SDG 3 requires global cooperation to provide equitable access to vaccines, medicines, and trained health professionals, particularly in vulnerable communities. Progress in this goal supports other SDGs, as health is foundational to economic growth, education, and poverty reduction.

Despite advancements, challenges like pandemics and unequal healthcare access persist, with only 17% of SDG targets on track as of 2023. By prioritizing health equity and resilience, SDG 3 seeks to create a world where everyone can thrive, leaving no one behind.
